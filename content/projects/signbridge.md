# SignBridge

SignBridge is an AI-powered sign language translator built by Sujib Shrestha and a team of three. The project won the OrchidHackX Hackathon.

## What it does

SignBridge uses computer vision and machine learning to recognize hand gestures in real time and translate them into text or speech output.

## Sujib's contribution

Sujib collaborated with the three-person team under hackathon time constraints and owned the recognition pipeline from prototype to demo.

Technology stack: TypeScript and Python.

Repository: https://github.com/DarkElixirRain/SignLanguage
